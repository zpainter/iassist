Guardians of The Digital Realm: Ensuring Trust In The Data Horizon*cancelled

The rapid expansion of data-driven ecosystems demands robust data governance technologies and platforms capable of building up trust, ensuring security, and promoting accountability across the entire data value chain. With increasing reliance of organisations on data for strategic decision-making, the need to manage data ethically, transparently, and securely has never been greater. This paper explores the evolving landscape of data governance technologies, with a focus on how blockchain, artificial intelligence, and cloud-based frameworks are setting new standards for data integrity and compliance. The paper reviews how these platforms allow for interoperability, automate the processes of compliance, and enhance traceability, thereby building institutional and public trust in the digital environment. In addition, the paper analyses how policy frameworks, metadata management, and audit mechanisms help foster accountability and ensure compliance with global data protection regulations. Data governance platforms can bridge the gap between innovation and regulation, supporting sustainable digital transformation, by integrating technological and institutional approaches. The study concludes that effective data governance entails socio-technical synergy that combines advanced technological capabilities with ethical stewardship and transparent accountability models. Such integration not only protects data assets but reinforces trust relationships among the stakeholders of the digital value chain.
