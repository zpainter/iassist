Turning Research Into A Story Editors Want To Read

Are you interested in publishing your work in IASSIST Quarterly or another academic journal? Curious about how to improve your chances of acceptance?

This session brings together experts with editorial experience to share practical strategies that successful authors can use to improve their drafts, increase their publication success, and reduce decision times.

We’ll begin with a discussion on article content and structure, including an overview of common article types and key considerations for each. The second presentation will focus on best practices for preparing your manuscript, including tips for properly formatting your paper before submission, communicating with the editorial team, and avoiding common mistakes that may get your paper rejected immediately.

Following these presentations, we’ll host an interactive Q&A session with the speakers, giving participants the opportunity to ask questions and receive personalized advice.

This session is designed for professionals seeking their first publication as well as experienced authors who want to deepen their understanding of editorial and peer-review processes.
