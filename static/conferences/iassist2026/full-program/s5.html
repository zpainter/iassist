Data Librarians Unleashed: Favorite Sources, Fierce Insights

Get ready for an exciting foray into the data landscape. This panel brings together highly experienced data librarians who will share a few of their favorite sources, from foundational datasets to lesser-known, specialized collections. The discussion will include materials with international perspectives and across many disciplines, offering insights that support diverse research needs. Attendees will gain practical strategies for locating and evaluating data. You’ll leave with a toolkit of resources, a dose of inspiration, and a renewed sense of what’s possible when you know where to look. Ideal for new and experienced data support professionals seeking to expand their data expertise.
