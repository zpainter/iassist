## Basic information about travelling to Vancouver, Canada

The information below is intended as a starting point. Travellers should confirm current requirements and guidelines directly with the Canadian government, as conditions may change.

### Arriving Vancouver

We encourage flying direct to Vancouver, British Columbia, from major cities in Europe, United States, and Canada. Direct flights to Vancouver International Airport (YVR) are available from most Canadian cities, from major hub cities in the United States and Europe (multiple options). [View airlines arriving in Vancouver](https://www.yvr.ca/en/passengers/flights/airlines-and-destinations).

Canadian airlines that fly from other Canadian cities to Vancouver:

* Air Canada  
* WestJet  
* Porter   
* Central Mountain Air  
* Pacific Coastal Airlines  
* Flair

All visitors entering Canada by air, land, and sea are required to carry a valid passport and in certain cases a visa. 

### Visa Application & Letters of Invitation - current as of November 2027

International travelers may need a Visa or Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) to enter Canada. [Find out if you need a Visa or eTA](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/check-visa-eta.html)

Find out more information about [applying for a visa to travel to Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/visitor-visa.html) or [applying for an eTA](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta/apply.html) if required.

Vancouver International Airport supports advance customs and immigration declaration. You can save time at the airport by completing your customs and immigration declaration in advance using the [ArriveCAN mobile or web app](https://www.canada.ca/en/border-services-agency/services/arrivecan.html) .

> Due to an increase in interest from attendees from countries that require visas, we find that we are unable to issue letters for visa support for all possible attendees. Therefore, we will be restricting letters of support to those who are **part of the program or acting in an official capacity** at the conference. We are sorry if this causes any inconvenience. If you have any questions, please contact us at programme ( at ) lists.iassistdata.org .

The Canadian government recommends that Visa applications are submitted at least 12 weeks prior to the potential travel. \[ Back to [IASSIST 2027 Vancouver, B.C., Canada main page](https://iassistdata.org/conferences/iassist2027) \]

### Transportation from London to Bristol

If you are flying into a London airport there are two main options for getting to Bristol, Train or Coach. 

The advantages of the train are that they usually run more frequently, and your time in transit tends to be shorter with it taking about 2.5 hours on the train from London to Bristol. Although we’d advise you do book, you have the option to book an open ticket or book a ticket at the train station.

There are no direct trains to Bristol from the airports so you will need to navigate to London Central before getting to Bristol. Information on how to do this for each airport can be found on their website.

The advantages of getting a coach, or bus to Bristol are that of price and convenience, busses leave directly from outside the airports, in most cases individual terminals have their own bus depo outside. National express coaches are usually rated the best, but there are also carriers Mega bus and Flix bus too.
