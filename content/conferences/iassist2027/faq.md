## Frequently asked questions related to the Conference

Frequently asked questions related to the Conference are answered here.

### Emergency Contact Details

911 is the emergency number in Canada. You should only call 911 if you are in an emergency situation and need immediate help.

If you are in a situation that is not an emergency (for example, lost identification or property), but you would still like to contact police, you can call the non-emergency line at 604-717-3321.

### Where will the conference take place?

The conference will take place at the Vancouver Campus of Simon Fraser University (Harbour Centre, 515 West Hastings St, Vancouver, BC) from May 26 – May 28, 2027.

### Where will the pre-conference workshops take place?

The pre-conference workshops will take place at the University of British Columbia (1961 East Mall, Vancouver, BC) on May 25, 2027.

### How to get to the conference venues from the Vancouver International Airport?

Getting to the conference venues situated in downtown Vancouver couldn’t be easier. The Canada Line takes you straight from the Airport to Waterstreet Station and steps from SFU Vancouver.

### Will there be an option for virtual attendance?

IASSIST 2027 will be an in-person only conference experience. In-person attendees must register online to attend the conference and participate in the workshops. Papers and presentations will be made available after the conference.

### What should I consider when planning my trip to Canada?

You should check in good time if you need a visa or electronic travel authorization (eTA) to travel to Canada. [Visit Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada.html) provides more information about [visa requirements](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/entry-requirements-country.html) and entering Canada.

For more information see [Visa and Travel](https://iassistdata.org/conferences/iassist2027/visa-and-travel/) page.

### What is the weather like in Vancouver in May?

May in Vancouver is a mild and pleasant. Conditions remain changeable but spring is transitioning into early summer. You can expect daytime highs around 16°C to 18°C (61°F to 64°F), occasionally warmer, and nighttime lows around 9°C to 11°C (48°F to 52°F). Flexible layers and a waterproof jacket are recommended because temperatures and conditions can shift quickly.
