Workshops will be held at the University of British Columbia Point Grey Campus, and the conference sessions will be at the Simon Fraser Vancouver Campus in downtown Vancouver.

Vancouver is a beautiful city with plenty of walking routes, parks and gardens, museums, and attractions.

### Local Attractions

Some attractions we suggest are: 

**Museums**	

- [UBC Museum of Anthropology](https://moa.ubc.ca/) - highly recommended museum with something of interest for everyone.   
- [Vancouver Art Gallery](https://www.vanartgallery.bc.ca/)  
- [Bill Reid Gallery](https://www.billreidgallery.ca/) of Northwest Coast Art - a smaller gallery of First Nations art, two blocks away from the Vancouver Art Gallery.  
- [Museum of Vancouver](https://museumofvancouver.ca/) - a museum about Vancouver itself. Next to the Planetarium.   
- [Vancouver Planetarium](https://www.spacecentre.ca/)

**Gardens and Walking**

- [Gastown](https://www.destinationvancouver.com/neighbourhoods/gastown) - one of the oldest neighbourhoods in Vancouver, with cobblestoned streets and the Gastown Steam Clock.  
- [Stanley Park](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/stanley-park.aspx) - Vancouver’s largest urban park. Includes the Vancouver Aquarium and is surrounded by the Sea Wall.   
- [Stanley Park Sea Wall](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/seawall.aspx) - beautiful views of Stanley Park and water. Easy paved walking.   
- [Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Gardens](https://vancouverchinesegarden.com/) - classical Chinese gardens. Tickets required.  
- [Queen Elizabeth Park and Gardens](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/queen-elizabeth-park.aspx)

**Libraries and Bookstores**

- [Vancouver Public Library](https://www.vpl.ca/hours-locations)  
- [Massy Books](https://www.massybooks.com/) - Indigenous owned and operated bookstore.   
- [Paper Hound Bookshop](https://paperhound.ca/)  
- [Iron Dog Books](https://irondogbooks.com/)

**Other Attractions and City Highlights**

- For sport events: check out what’s on at [BC Place](https://www.bcplace.com/),[Rogers Arena,](https://rogersarena.com/) and [Nat Bailey Stadium](https://www.milb.com/vancouver/ballpark). Vancouver hosts the last MLB affiliate baseball team, the Vancouver Canadians who are associated with the Toronto Blue Jays. Hockey season runs roughly from October to the end of June.   
- [Cycling routes and maps](https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/cycling-routes-maps-and-trip-planner.aspx)  
- There are options for seaplane tours of Vancouver and whale options for a fee.   
- Vancouver’s Shakespeare Festival,[Bard on the Beach](https://bardonthebeach.org/whats-on/), runs from June forward.  
- For m[ore suggestions, check out the City of Vancouver’s website](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/attractions-and-leisure.aspx).
