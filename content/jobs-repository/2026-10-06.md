**Data Curation Specialist for Humanities and Social Sciences**

The Data Curation Specialist for Humanities and Social Sciences advances the mission of the Library through working with faculty, students and staff to help manage, share and preserve their research data and other content of scholarly value, with a specialized focus on Humanities and Social Sciences. This is a highly collaborative position requiring coordination and negotiation of relationships and responsibilities across the Library and the University.

You will be an important member of the Deep Blue Repository & Research Data Services (DBRRDS) team and contribute to all aspects of its work. You will help develop data management and sharing plans, consult with faculty, staff, and students on their scholarly communication needs in collaboration with librarian experts and subject specialists, provide training to librarians and researchers on managing and sharing their scholarship and research data, and help researchers share their scholarship and research data with others through the library's [Deep Blue Documents](https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/documents) and [Deep Blue Data](https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/data/) repositories.

You will be the primary service contact for DBRRDS with Humanities and Social Sciences faculty, staff and students at U-M's Ann Arbor, Dearborn and Flint campuses. Other service activities and functions may be negotiated depending upon your knowledge, expertise, and interest.

You will report directly to the Director of Deep Blue Repository & Research Data Services under the Associate University Librarian for Publishing.

You may supervise student workers.

**What You'll Do**

While partnering with colleagues at the Library, U-M Office of Research, Information Technology Services, Advanced Research Computing, as well as selected academic programs, institutes, departments, schools, and colleges across U-M campuses, you will engage in:

**Consultation (20%)**

You will use your knowledge of best practices in document and data curation and its potential application in different research settings to serve as a consultant for faculty and students in Humanities and Social Sciences fields. Consultation activities will range from single meetings to address a particular need to longer-term collaborations with researchers to explore or address larger issues. This may include participating in funded research projects as a named partner, depending on the interests, expertise and capacity of the person in this position.

**Education and Community Development (25%)**

You will identify and connect with faculty and students who are engaged or interested in curating data and scholarly content from Humanities and Social Sciences fields. You will offer workshops and other educational programming on introductory and specialized topics of interest to the U-M community. You will also coordinate or lead meetings, events, or projects designed to facilitate communication, connections, and engagement between faculty, staff, students, and research centers across the U-M campuses.

**Development of Local Standards, Tools and Support (20%)**

Using your knowledge of best practices in curation, as well as the needs of U-M researchers, you will design, develop and implement local standards of practice for the Library and the U M community to apply in support of advancing data curation and document deposit workflows. You will serve as the main liaison to the Rackham Graduate School and coordinate its dissertation deposit workflows. You will also design technical and policy tools that are practical and easy to use to facilitate and enhance the data and scholarly content produced by the research being done at U-M. This may include pursuing external funding depending on the expertise, interest and capacity of the person in this position.

**Data and Digital Curation (15%)**

You will review data sets submitted to Deep Blue Data, in conjunction with other experts as needed, to assess the condition of the data set and to make recommendations to enhance the value and accessibility of the data for others. You will communicate recommendations to the researcher to address gaps, improve the value of the data's findability, accessibility, interoperability and reusability (FAIR), and enhance the data's potential for reproducibility. You will serve as a data curator in the [Data Curation Network](https://datacuration.network), a consortium of academic libraries offering data curation and repository services.

**Service Response (10%)**

In conjunction with the other data curators, you will monitor and resolve service requests received for Deep Blue Documents and Deep Blue Data. These requests may range from supporting depositors to reaching out to authors to release their work upon request. You will also work with the developers of Deep Blue Documents to provide reports to collection administrators upon request.

**Professional Development and Service (10%)**

You will pursue enrichment and professional development activities as appropriate to the position and individual interests. You will also engage and contribute to the library, disciplinary, and other relevant communities of practice.

**Skills You Have**

- An advanced degree (Masters or PhD level) in a relevant academic discipline or a Master's Degree in Library Science.
- One (1) year direct experience with the information technologies and best practices prevalent in research data management, sharing, and/or curation.
- One (1) year combined experience with and/or education with at least one type of data. This could include qualitative data, observational data, experimental data, code, etc.
- Demonstrated commitment to protecting the integrity of sensitive data (including balancing openness and protection) as well as compliance with relevant regulations and adherence to professional ethics.
- Familiarity with the FAIR and CARE principles and the necessity of making research accessible for the benefit of the global community.
- Demonstrated commitment to fostering a welcoming environment by recognizing and incorporating differing viewpoints, backgrounds, and ideas to enrich the organization.
- Motivation to provide excellent service and to collaboratively improve workflows and processes.
- Excellent written and oral communication skills and an ability to present and share ideas clearly and effectively to diverse audiences.

**You May Also Have**

- One (1) year combined experience and/or education with at least one type of Humanities and/or Social Sciences data. This could include qualitative data, observational data, experimental data, code, etc.
- Experience supporting researcher information needs and creating effective responses to meet those needs in a service.
- Experience working in an academic library.
- Experience working with data repositories.
- Experience working through the nuances of sensitive data, including ethical issues and compliance with journal and funding agency mandates.

(Note: desired qualifications above are not required to apply and be considered for the position).
